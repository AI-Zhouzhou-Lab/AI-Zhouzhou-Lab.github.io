Our paper, "AGHRNet: An attention ghost-HRNet for confirmation of catch-and-shake locations in jujube fruits vibration harvesting," has been selected as an ESI Highly Cited Paper. This research marks significant progress in visual perception for key areas in winter jujube vibration harvesting.

The National Key R&D Program project "Breeding of New High-quality and High-yield Varieties of Jujube and Torreya and Precision Cultivation Technology" (2022YFD2200404) (in which our lab is a participant) has officially launched. The project period is from 2022 to 2027.
