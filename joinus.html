Join Us !

We are actively recruiting self-motivated Master's and PhD students, postdoctoral researchers, and research interns with a strong background in agricultural engineering, agricultural robotics, machine vision, and deep learning.

Our research group focuses on next-generation smart agriculture, specifically in orchard harvesting robots, multi-modal sensing, and AI-driven methodologies. We provide a dynamic and highly supportive environment for your academic and professional growth.

If you are interested in joining us, please send your CV and a brief statement of research interests to Prof. Zhouzhou Zheng via email at zhengzz@hunau.edu.cn with the subject line:

'Prospective [Position] – [Your Name]'
