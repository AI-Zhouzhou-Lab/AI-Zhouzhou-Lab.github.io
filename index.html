Zhouzhou Zheng, Ph.D.

Lecturer, Master's Supervisor

Department of Agricultural Engineering, Hunan Agricultural University

I am a Lecturer in the College of Mechanical and Electrical Engineering at Hunan Agricultural University. I received my Ph.D. degree from the College of Mechanical and Electronic Engineering at Northwest A&F University in 2025. From 2023 to 2024, I was a joint Ph.D. student at McGill University, Canada, funded by the China Scholarship Council (CSC). My main research interests are to develop and adopt innovative sensing technologies and AI-driven computational methodologies for solving challenges in next-generation smart agriculture, specifically focusing on orchard harvesting robots.

Specifically, my research interests include:

  • Orchard harvesting robots and catch-and-shake strategies.
  • Multi-modal sensor fusion and complex environment target detection.
  • Fruit tree phenotyping based on deep learning.
  • Vibration harvesting mechanism and equipment optimization for oil tea.
  • Agricultural robotics and automation.
