Zhouzhou Zheng (郑洲洲)

Zhouzhou Zheng is a Lecturer and Master's Supervisor at the College of Mechanical and Electrical Engineering, Hunan Agricultural University. He received his Ph.D. in Agricultural Mechanization Engineering from Northwest A&F University in June 2025. From 2023 to 2024, he was a joint Ph.D. student at McGill University in Canada, funded by the China Scholarship Council (CSC).

His research focuses on orchard harvesting robots, orchard information perception technology, and fruit tree phenotyping. He has published over 10 SCI papers as the first author in top journals such as Computers and Electronics in Agriculture and Expert Systems with Applications, including 6 papers in JCR Q1 with a cumulative impact factor of 87.1.

Yujie Wu

Yujie Wu received his Bachelor's degree in Mechatronic Engineering from Shangqiu Institute of Technology. His current research direction is agricultural computer vision, focusing on monitoring the growth cycle of rapeseed in complex field environments based on image enhancement and segmentation techniques. He is dedicated to integrating his mechatronics engineering background with deep learning algorithms to overcome complex field illumination and occlusion interference, achieving accurate perception and analysis of rapeseed growth status.

Junyu Liu

Junyu Liu received his Bachelor's degree in Network Engineering from Xizang Minzu University and is currently a Master's student in Mechanical Engineering at Hunan Agricultural University. He currently focuses on the research and development of agricultural robotic arm recognition and harvesting robots. He specializes in visual perception and robotic arm control, capable of building a vision-robotic arm collaboration system based on ROS2 to complete target recognition and positioning, hand-eye calibration, grasping pose estimation, and motion control. He also has experience in STM32 microcontroller development, peripheral driver, and actuator control, capable of integrating the system from visual recognition, control algorithms to embedded execution endpoints, dedicated to improving the recognition accuracy and operational efficiency of agricultural harvesting robots.
