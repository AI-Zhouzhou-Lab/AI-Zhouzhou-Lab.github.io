Zhouzhou Zheng (郑洲洲)

Zhouzhou Zheng is a Lecturer and Master's Supervisor at the College of Mechanical and Electrical Engineering, Hunan Agricultural University. He received his Ph.D. in Agricultural Mechanization Engineering from Northwest A&F University in June 2025. From 2023 to 2024, he was a joint Ph.D. student at McGill University in Canada, funded by the China Scholarship Council (CSC).

His research focuses on orchard harvesting robots, orchard information perception technology, and fruit tree phenotyping. He has published over 10 SCI papers as the first author in top journals such as Computers and Electronics in Agriculture and Expert Systems with Applications, including 6 papers in JCR Q1 with a cumulative impact factor of 87.1.

Biao Chen

Biao Chen received his Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Design, Manufacturing and Automation from the Oriental College of Science and Technology, Hunan Agricultural University. He is currently pursuing a Master's degree in Agricultural Engineering and Information Technology (Agricultural Machinery and Intelligent Equipment) at the College of Mechanical and Electrical Engineering, Hunan Agricultural University. His main research focuses on the vibration harvesting of Camellia oleifera, with a particular emphasis on the vibration-induced abscission mechanism of Camellia oleifera fruits. He is dedicated to overcoming the biological characteristic of "flower and fruit coexisting" in Camellia oleifera and its geographical characteristics of being mostly planted in hilly and mountainous areas, aiming to develop a single-person portable handheld vibration harvesting device for Camellia oleifera.

Yujie Wu

Yujie Wu received her Bachelor's degree in Mechatronic Engineering from Shangqiu Institute of Technology. She is currently a Master's student in Agricultural Engineering and Information Technology at Hunan Agricultural University. Her current research direction is agricultural computer vision, focusing on monitoring the growth cycle of rapeseed in complex field environments based on image enhancement and segmentation techniques. She is dedicated to integrating her mechatronics engineering background with deep learning algorithms to overcome complex field illumination and occlusion interference, achieving accurate perception and analysis of rapeseed growth status.

Wenbiao Tian

Wenbiao Tian received his Bachelor's degree in Agricultural Mechanization and Automation from Hunan Agricultural University and is currently a Master's student in Agricultural Engineering and Information Technology at the same university. His current research focuses on the intelligentization of agricultural equipment, specifically the system integration of embedded software and hardware with visual recognition and parameter perception technologies. He is dedicated to integrating his agricultural mechanization engineering background with embedded system development to overcome field illumination variations and machine vibration interference, thereby achieving intelligent operation and efficiency improvement from visual recognition and parameter perception to the execution end.

Junjie Zhou

Junjie Zhou received his Bachelor's degree in Agricultural Mechanization and Automation from Hunan Agricultural University. He is currently a Master's student in Mechanical Engineering at the same university. His current research mainly focuses on Camellia oleifera fruit vibration harvesting equipment, specifically the design of harvesting mechanisms based on mechanical vibration principles. He is dedicated to integrating his mechanical design engineering background with field harvesting requirements to overcome the challenges of difficult detachment and high damage rates in Camellia oleifera harvesting, achieving efficient and low-damage mechanized harvesting.

Junyu Liu

Junyu Liu received his Bachelor's degree in Network Engineering from Xizang Minzu University and is currently a Master's student in Agricultural Engineering and Information Technology at Hunan Agricultural University. His current research mainly focuses on visual perception and robotic arm control. He builds a vision-robotic arm collaboration system based on ROS2 to complete target recognition and positioning, grasping pose estimation, and motion control. He has experience in STM32 microcontroller development, peripheral driver, and actuator control, and is capable of integrating the system from visual recognition, control algorithms to embedded execution endpoints. He is dedicated to improving the recognition accuracy and operational efficiency of agricultural harvesting robots.
