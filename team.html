Zhouzhou Zheng (郑洲洲)

Zhouzhou Zheng is a Lecturer and Master's Supervisor at the College of Mechanical and Electrical Engineering, Hunan Agricultural University. He received his Ph.D. in Agricultural Mechanization Engineering from Northwest A&F University in June 2025. From 2023 to 2024, he was a joint Ph.D. student at McGill University in Canada, funded by the China Scholarship Council (CSC).

His research focuses on orchard harvesting robots, orchard information perception technology, and fruit tree phenotyping. He has published over 10 SCI papers as the first author in top journals such as Computers and Electronics in Agriculture and Expert Systems with Applications, including 6 papers in JCR Q1 with a cumulative impact factor of 87.1. He serves as a youth editorial board member for Plant Phenomics and a guest editor for Agronomy and Sustainability.
